# Brief (scroll-craft)

Interviewed across the conversation of 2026-09-28 to 2026-09-30. User answers are paraphrased only where noted.

## The eight topics
1. **Vibe:** smooth, immersive, museum-like, cinematic, refined. Reference for rhythm only: Mammut Eiger Extreme.
2. **Journey (user):** progressively discovering information about dinosaurs; photographic image, solid white, dark green, photographic image.
3. **Energy curve:** quiet intro and statement, rising through the exhibits, loudest at the bite, calm resolved ending.
4. **Feeling and the one moment:** see below.
5. **What no other site does:** you scroll through geological time; images decode out of the ASCII ground they are buried in.
6. **Range:** editorial, premium, Awwwards-level, not generic.
7. **One world or scenes:** distinct scenes (chapters), connected by a trackway and a depth gauge.
8. **Assets:** six supplied illustrations (one duplicate), no video.

## Grammar
**Exhibition chapters.** Why not the others: filmic one-shot needs continuous footage (none); a product grid does not tell a story; a gallery would lose the time axis. The chapters grammar allows hard cuts between photography and flat color, which is what the brief asks for.

**Peak:** "The page's frame opened like jaws and the number 35,000 newtons filled the screen." It lives in The bite, which has the longest pinned span (180 percent) and follows the quiet Senses chapter.

**Tell-someone sentence:** "It's the site where you dig down through time and every fossil decodes out of the ground as you reach it."

**Authored silence:** the empty moment after the hero sinks, and the flat moss statement. Both are intentional, not dead scroll.

## Score: one device per beat
| Section | Device family | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | timeline sequence | Entering time needs a clock, not a spinner |
| Hero | depth layers + decode | Real planes give the first impression weight; decode is the brand signature |
| Statement | kinetic type (scrubbed lines) | Text-only beat, the words are the image |
| Discovery | clip reveal (slit) | A display case opening is a change of state |
| Senses | pin + depixelate + zoom | Perception is about resolution and focus |
| Bite | pin + shape clip (jaws) | The reveal is literally the subject |
| Sue | counters + stepped bars | Evidence should feel measured |
| Anatomy | type mask + scan | Seeing inside the body |
| Relatives | decode (bookend) | Returns to the signature, closing the loop |

Families used: 8. No family twice in a row. Pinned sections on desktop only: hero, senses, bite, anatomy, never adjacent without an unpinned beat between (senses and bite are separated by a scroll-in threshold, and mobile pins nothing).

## Motion justification (one sentence each)
- Background color changes: each chapter is a different room of the hall.
- ASCII field fades on flat color: solid chapters are quiet rooms.
- Trackway: guides the eye across diagonal cuts between chapters.
- Depth gauge: tells the reader where they are in time.
- Hero pointer parallax: proves the head is a separate object in space.
